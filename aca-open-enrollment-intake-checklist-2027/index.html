A calmer first call for ACA open enrollment

A 2027 Marketplace intake checklist for insurance agents covering state, household changes, plan priorities, and a clear next step.

By AllCalls EditorialEdition Nov 3, 2026

An ACA caller might be between shifts or standing outside a noisy job site. A good intake puts the essential questions in a predictable order and lets the caller decide when they have time to go further.

Start with location and coverage

Ask what state the person lives in and whether they have Marketplace coverage now. Some states operate their own marketplaces. The CMS state exchange directory is a practical starting point for routing the call.

Ask what has changed since the last application. The answer could involve expected income, household members, a move, or an offer of work-based coverage. HealthCare.gov has a list of changes to report. Let the caller describe their situation before asking for details that belong in an approved enrollment workflow.

Find out what they need the plan to do

Some callers lead with the premium. Others need to keep a clinic, medication, or hospital close to home. Write down the concern to verify, confirm what plan and year you’re checking, and tell the caller where the information comes from.

Make the handoff easy to repeat

Before ending the call, agree on a next action and repeat it in a sentence the caller can use with a family member. For Marketplace assistance, use your approved process to document consumer consent and their review of application accuracy where required. The CMS model consent form describes both steps. Track completion separately from the call outcome so nobody confuses a good conversation with a finished application.
