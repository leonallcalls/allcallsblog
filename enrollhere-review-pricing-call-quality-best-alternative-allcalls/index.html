EnrollHere Review: Pricing, Call Quality & Best Alternative (AllCalls)

By ShawnApr 3, 2026

If you’ve been researching inbound insurance leads, you’ve probably come across EnrollHere. It’s one of the more recognized names in the health and life insurance lead space — but is it actually worth it for independent agents and agencies?

This EnrollHere review breaks down everything you need to know: pricing, call quality, transparency, and how it compares to the best alternative on the market right now.

What Is EnrollHere?

EnrollHere is an insurance lead generation platform that connects agents with consumers who are actively shopping for health, life, and ACA coverage. The platform sources leads primarily through digital advertising and routes them to agents either as form fills, live transfers, or inbound calls depending on the product.

It’s marketed as a simple, ready-to-go lead source — you sign up, fund your account, and start receiving leads. For agents who don’t want to manage their own marketing, that plug-and-play appeal is real.

But simplicity comes at a cost.

EnrollHere Pricing: What Do Leads Actually Cost?

EnrollHere pricing isn’t publicly listed, which is already a transparency red flag for agents trying to forecast their cost per acquisition.

Based on agent reports and industry benchmarks, EnrollHere lead costs typically fall in these ranges:

  • ACA / Health leads: $20–$45 per lead
  • Life insurance leads: $15–$35 per lead
  • Live transfers / inbound calls: $40–$80+ depending on market and exclusivity

The problem isn’t just the price — it’s the lack of control. EnrollHere doesn’t offer full bidding control, meaning you can’t easily adjust what you’re paying based on performance data. You’re largely at the mercy of their pricing model.

For agents who want to optimize cost per acquisition over time, that’s a serious limitation.

EnrollHere Call Quality: Is It Worth It?

Call quality is where EnrollHere gets the most mixed reviews from agents in the field.

What agents report:

  • Call intent varies significantly — some callers are actively shopping, others are exploratory or confused about what they signed up for
  • Shared leads are common, meaning multiple agents may be working the same caller
  • Speed to lead is inconsistent — by the time you’re connected, the caller may have already spoken to a competitor
  • Return and credit policies can be frustrating to navigate

To be fair, no lead platform delivers a 100% close-ready caller every time. But the shared lead model and limited routing transparency make it harder to build a predictable, scalable pipeline with EnrollHere.

EnrollHere Pros and Cons

Pros:

  • Easy to get started — low setup friction
  • Covers ACA, health, and life verticals
  • Decent call volume in competitive markets
  • No long-term contracts reported by most users

Cons:

  • Limited pricing control and bidding transparency
  • Shared leads reduce your competitive edge
  • Call quality is inconsistent across markets
  • Difficult to scale efficiently without overpaying
  • Routing transparency is below industry standard

Is EnrollHere Legit?

Yes — EnrollHere is a legitimate platform. It’s not a scam. Agents do receive leads and calls through the platform, and some agents in certain markets report decent results.

But “legit” and “the right fit for your agency” are two different things.

Agents who are newer to inbound calls and want a simple starting point may find EnrollHere workable. Agents who are serious about scaling, controlling cost per acquisition, and building a predictable book of business will quickly run into the platform’s ceiling.
