Medicare & ACA

2027 open enrollment has two clocks

Medicare open enrollment ends December 7. On HealthCare.gov, December 15 is the cutoff for January 1 coverage. For one household, that distinction can change when coverage starts.

By AllCalls EditorialEdition Nov 25, 2026

The most important question on a late-November call may be the simplest one: “Which coverage are we talking about?” Medicare and the Affordable Care Act Marketplace are both in open enrollment for 2027, but their December dates mean different things. A caller who hears “December 15” on a Marketplace ad can easily assume it applies to a parent’s Medicare plan.

Put the program before the date

ProgramDateWhat it means
Medicare annual open enrollmentDecember 7, 2026Last day of the annual period to make changes for January 1, 2027.
HealthCare.govDecember 15, 2026Enroll or change plans by this date for January 1 coverage, with the first premium paid.
HealthCare.govJanuary 15, 2027Federal Marketplace open enrollment ends. Selections made December 16 through January 15 generally start February 1 if the first premium is paid.

Confirm the state before quoting a Marketplace deadline. Some states run their own exchanges, and their calendars can differ. For Medicare, start with the caller’s current coverage and the specific issue they want to resolve. CMS lists the state exchanges, and Medicare Plan Finder lets people check local health and drug plan options.

Give the household one next step

Imagine an adult daughter at a kitchen table helping her mother read a plan letter. She may also be shopping for her own Marketplace coverage after a change at work. Those are two separate conversations. Say which program applies to each person, what date matters, and which piece of information they should find before the next call. Repeating the dates without naming the programs only gives the two of them more to remember.

Build the same distinction into your intake notes. Start with the program, confirm the state or ZIP code, and end with who will do what by when. The ACA intake checklist gives agents a short sequence for that side of the conversation. If the caller names a medication or specialist they rely on, check the current plan information and explain the source you used. Build in room to say that a question needs a second look.

Official dates appear in the CMS 2027 Medicare enrollment announcement and the HealthCare.gov calendar.
