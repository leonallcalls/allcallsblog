Quick Comparison Table

AllCalls.io: Best Overall

AllCalls.io is the premier choice for Final Expense agents who need a reliable, on-demand stream of live inbound calls without the burden of contracts. Unlike traditional lead vendors that require pre-scheduled blocks of time, this platform allows agents to toggle their availability “on” or “off” instantly through a mobile app or desktop dashboard. Ringba: Best for Enterprise Agencies

Ringba is a sophisticated call tracking and routing platform designed for large-scale Final Expense operations that manage their own marketing campaigns. It offers unparalleled granularity in how calls are distributed, allowing managers to set complex rules based on agent performance, geography, or time of day. For agencies spending over $10,000 monthly on lead generation, Ringba provides the data necessary to optimize ROI across multiple traffic sources.

  • Key Features: Real-time reporting, Instant Caller Profile data, and advanced IVR (Interactive Voice Response) builders.
  • Pros: Extremely powerful routing logic; integrates with almost any CRM; robust fraud prevention tools.
  • Cons: Steep learning curve for solo agents; requires a monthly software subscription fee plus usage.
  • Pricing: Tiered monthly subscription starting at $0 (usage-only) to $1,000+ for enterprise features.
  • Best For: Large agencies and lead aggregators who need to route thousands of calls daily.

CallTools: Best for Hybrid Outbound/Inbound

CallTools is an ideal solution for Final Expense agents who supplement their live transfers with outbound prospecting. It functions primarily as a cloud-based contact center, offering a predictive dialer that can switch seamlessly to an inbound queue. This ensures that when an agent isn’t receiving a live transfer from a platform like AllCalls.io, they can remain productive by dialing their existing database of aged leads.

  • Key Features: Predictive dialing, preview dialing, and built-in CRM functionality.
  • Pros: Excellent for teams that do both cold calling and inbound; high-quality audio; reliable uptime.
  • Cons: Monthly per-seat pricing can be expensive for solo users; requires a consistent list of data to dial.
  • Pricing: Per-agent monthly subscription (typically $100-$150 per seat).
  • Best For: Agencies with 3+ seats that maintain an active outbound sales strategy.

Convoso: Best for High-Volume Contact Centers

Convoso is a lead generation platform that focuses on maximizing agent productivity through advanced automation. Their “ClearView” dashboard provides real-time insights into which lead sources are performing best for Final Expense scripts. While heavily focused on outbound, their inbound routing capabilities are designed to minimize “dead air” and ensure agents are always connected to a live human.

  • Key Features: Answering machine detection, multi-channel communication (SMS/Email), and dynamic scripting.
  • Pros: Industry-leading answering machine detection; scales easily for 20+ agents; aggressive dialing algorithms.
  • Cons: Can be “too much” software for a single independent agent; higher price point than competitors.
  • Pricing: Custom enterprise pricing based on volume and seat count.
  • Best For: Large Final Expense call centers focusing on high-volume lead penetration.

Retreaver: Best for Data-Driven Routing

Retreaver specializes in “tag-based” call routing, which is highly effective for Final Expense agents who need to know exactly what a consumer was looking at before they called. By tagging callers with specific data points (like age or state), Retreaver ensures the call is routed to the agent best qualified to close that specific demographic. This level of data transparency helps in maintaining high compliance standards in the insurance industry.

  • Key Features: Real-time data post-back, dynamic phone number insertion, and deep CRM integrations.
  • Pros: Exceptional data transparency; easy to track which marketing campaigns are driving the best calls.
  • Cons: Requires technical knowledge to set up “tags” and tracking pixels; usage-based fees can add up.
  • Pricing: Usage-based pricing with monthly minimums.
  • Best For: Tech-savvy agents who want to track the exact journey of their Final Expense leads.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between inbound calls and aged leads?

Inbound calls connect you with a consumer who is currently on the phone looking for a quote, whereas aged leads are contact details of people who expressed interest days or months ago. Inbound calls generally have a 3x higher intent level because the consumer is initiating the contact during their peak buying window.

How much do inbound Final Expense calls cost in 2026?

Prices typically range from $45 to $85 per billable call in 2026, depending on call duration and filtering. Platforms like AllCalls.io use a pay-per-call model, ensuring you only pay for calls that meet a specific billable duration.

Can I filter inbound calls by state?

Yes, modern platforms allow agents to select exactly which states they are licensed in. AllCalls.io provides a real-time dashboard where agents can toggle states on or off instantly to ensure they only receive calls where they are legally authorized to sell.

Is a contract required for live transfer platforms?

While some enterprise platforms require monthly subscriptions, on-demand platforms like AllCalls.io do not require long-term contracts. This allows agents to pay for leads as they go and stop the service at any time without penalty.
