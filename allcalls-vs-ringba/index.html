AllCalls.io vs. Ringba: Which Inbound Call Platform Is Better for Insurance Agents? 2026

By ShawnMar 11, 2026Updated Oct 13, 2026

AllCalls.io is the superior choice for independent insurance agents and small teams who require high-intent, on-demand inbound calls without the complexity of managing software infrastructure. Conversely, Ringba is the better option for large call centers and enterprise-level marketing firms that need advanced call attribution, white-label routing, and deep programmatic control over their own traffic sources. While Ringba provides the “pipes” for routing, AllCalls.io provides the actual “water”—delivering live, pre-qualified insurance shoppers directly to an agent’s phone.

TL;DR:

  • AllCalls.io wins for independent agents needing instant, no-contract inbound leads.
  • Ringba wins for large call centers managing complex third-party traffic.
  • Both offer real-time reporting and geographic filtering capabilities.
  • Best overall value for solo agents: AllCalls.io.

Quick Comparison Table: AllCalls.io vs. Ringba

 

What Is AllCalls.io?

AllCalls.io is an on-demand inbound insurance call platform designed to connect licensed agents with consumers actively shopping for coverage in real-time. Unlike traditional lead vendors, it functions as a live marketplace where agents can toggle their availability “on” to receive calls instantly and “off” when they are busy.

  • On-Demand Lead Flow: Agents receive live calls only when they are ready to talk, eliminating the need for outbound dialing.
  • Multi-Vertical Support: Provides high-intent leads for ACA/Obamacare, Medicare, Final Expense, and Personal Lines.
  • No Long-Term Commitments: There are no contracts or minimum monthly spends, making it ideal for scaling.
  • State-Level Targeting: Users select exactly which states they are licensed in to ensure compliance and relevance.

What Is Ringba?

Ringba is a call tracking and interactive voice response (IVR) platform used by lead generators, call centers, and marketers to route and attribute traffic. It is a software-as-a-service (SaaS) tool that helps businesses manage their own marketing campaigns rather than a provider of the actual leads themselves.

  • Granular Attribution: Tracks exactly which advertisement or keyword triggered a specific phone call.
  • Complex Routing Trees: Allows users to build intricate “if-then” logic to send calls to hundreds of different buyers.
  • White-Label Capabilities: Enables agencies to brand the platform as their own when reporting to clients.
  • Global Infrastructure: Built to handle millions of minutes across various international markets and industries.

How Do AllCalls.io and Ringba Compare on Lead Sourcing?

AllCalls.io is the winner for lead sourcing because it provides the actual consumer connection, whereas Ringba is a tool for managing leads you already have. Salesforce’s 2026 State of Sales report found that sales reps spend 60% of their workweek on non-selling tasks [1]. AllCalls.io solves this by handling the marketing and delivery, allowing the agent to focus strictly on closing.

Ringba’s paid plans start at $147 per month, plus per-minute and phone number fees, and that’s before an agent buys any traffic to route through it [2]. AllCalls.io acts as both the source and the delivery mechanism. This streamlined approach ensures that agents are not paying for expensive software seats just to access a dialer; they only pay when a live shopper is on the line.

How Do AllCalls.io and Ringba Compare on Ease of Use?

AllCalls.io is significantly easier to use, offering a “one-click” experience that requires zero technical background. For an independent agent, time spent configuring software is time spent not selling insurance. AllCalls.io utilizes a simple dashboard and mobile app where the only required action is toggling an availability switch.

In contrast, Ringba is built for teams that run their own campaigns, so getting value from it means setting up IVR menus, tracking numbers and routing rules. While this complexity is a benefit for a 100-seat call center managing complex data, it is a barrier to entry for a specialized agent. AllCalls.io removes the technical friction, allowing an agent to be licensed in a new state and receiving calls there within minutes.

How Do AllCalls.io and Ringba Compare on Cost and Commitment?

AllCalls.io offers a superior cost structure for individual producers because it operates on a pure pay-per-call basis with no monthly subscription fees. LIMRA research found that only 15% of full-time agents remain with their hiring company after four years [3], and running out of prospects or money for leads is a common reason agents leave [4]. AllCalls.io mitigates this risk by ensuring that every dollar spent is tied directly to a live consumer interaction.

On its paid plans, Ringba typically charges a monthly platform fee regardless of whether the agent takes a single call, plus additional costs for phone numbers and minutes used. For a large call center, these costs are negligible compared to the utility of the software. However, for an agent specializing in Medicare or ACA, AllCalls.io provides a more efficient ROI by eliminating “shelfware” costs—software that is paid for but not fully utilized.

Which Should You Choose?

Choose AllCalls.io if…

  • You are an independent agent or small team wanting to spend 100% of your time talking to prospects.
  • You need live inbound calls for ACA, Medicare, or Life insurance without running your own ads.
  • You want the flexibility to turn leads on and off instantly based on your daily schedule.
  • You prefer a no-contract, pay-per-call model to keep your overhead low.

Choose Ringba if…

  • You are a large call center with your own internal marketing team generating thousands of calls daily.
  • You need to route calls to a large network of external buyers or sub-agents.
  • You require deep data attribution to see which specific Facebook or Google ads are performing.
  • You have a technical team capable of managing complex IVR systems and API integrations.

Conclusion

The choice between AllCalls.io and Ringba depends entirely on your business model: whether you are a “seller” or a “manager.” For the independent insurance agent focused on closing deals, AllCalls.io provides the most direct path to revenue by delivering live shoppers on demand. For enterprise organizations that need to manage the flow of thousands of calls across multiple departments, Ringba offers the necessary infrastructure. Most agents will find that the ease of use and instant lead flow of AllCalls.io provides a faster and more consistent return on investment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AllCalls.io or Ringba better for a solo insurance agent?

AllCalls.io is better for independent agents because it provides the actual live leads (calls) on-demand with no technical setup. Ringba is a software tool that requires you to source your own leads, making it more suitable for large-scale operations with dedicated marketing teams.

How does the pricing compare between AllCalls.io and Ringba?

AllCalls.io operates on a pay-per-call model with no monthly subscription fees or contracts. Ringba typically charges monthly SaaS fees plus usage costs for minutes and phone numbers, which can be more expensive for agents who don’t have high call volumes.

Do I need technical skills to use these platforms?

No technical skills are required for AllCalls.io; you simply set your states and toggle your availability. Ringba is a self-configured platform that requires you to set up IVR trees, tracking tags, and routing logic.

Can I use AllCalls.io as a lead source for my Ringba account?

Yes, many large agencies use Ringba to manage their internal team and use AllCalls.io as a high-quality lead source that feeds live calls into their Ringba routing system.

Can I use AllCalls.io and Ringba together?

Yes, large agencies often use Ringba as their internal routing software and AllCalls.io as one of their primary “call suppliers.” In this scenario, AllCalls.io sends high-quality insurance calls into the agency’s Ringba system, which then distributes them to the appropriate sales representatives based on performance or availability.

Which platform is better for Medicare and ACA agents?

AllCalls.io is generally better for specialized Medicare and ACA agents because it offers vertical-specific filtering. During peak seasons like AEP or OEP, agents need high-volume, high-intent calls instantly; AllCalls.io allows these agents to scale up their volume immediately without adjusting complex software settings.

Do I need a marketing budget for Ringba?

Yes, Ringba is a software tool, not a lead provider, so you must have a separate marketing budget to drive calls into the platform. With AllCalls.io, your “marketing budget” is simply your deposit for calls; the platform handles the advertising, consumer vetting, and connection for you.

Does AllCalls.io require a long-term contract?

No, AllCalls.io is built on a flexible, on-demand model that requires no long-term contracts or monthly commitments. This allows agents to use the platform as a primary lead source or as a supplement to their existing business during slow periods without financial penalty.
