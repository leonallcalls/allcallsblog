The 2027 Medicare plan check starts with the medicine cabinet

A practical Medicare open enrollment checklist for agents who need to verify prescriptions, pharmacies, providers, and costs with callers.

By AllCalls EditorialEdition Oct 16, 2026

When a caller wants to know whether they should review their Medicare coverage, ask what their plan needs to cover on an ordinary week. That question can surface a specialist they see monthly, a neighborhood pharmacy they can walk to, or a prescription they cannot miss.

Work from a current list

Ask the caller to gather the name and dosage of regular prescriptions and the pharmacies they use. Note the clinicians or hospitals that matter most. If a provider name is common, use the office location to confirm you mean the same practice.

Use the caller’s location and the 2027 plan year when checking Medicare Plan Finder. The plan’s current documents can help clarify coverage and costs. If the caller has a plan letter, start with the Annual Notice of Change. If the directory and provider office give different answers, record the discrepancy and verify it before telling the caller what to expect.

Compare a year of costs and access

Review premiums alongside likely out-of-pocket costs and any limits that matter to the caller. The cheapest monthly option can feel different once a household considers the trips it makes, the prescriptions it fills, and the providers it sees. The CMS open enrollment resource explains why costs, coverage, and networks deserve a yearly review.

Close by naming what you verified, what remains open, and when you’ll reconnect. Medicare’s fall open enrollment runs from October 15 through December 7. For a caller weighing several options, a callback with a specific answer can make those weeks much easier to navigate.
